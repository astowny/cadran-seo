§ 01 — Findings

Your trade is not the problem. Your site does not speak to Google.

A WordPress site set up with no SEO work is a shop sign on a street nobody walks down. Here is what we find in nine audits out of ten.

01

A heavy theme that loses visitors

Four to six seconds to load on a phone. Half your visitors close the page before your phone number ever appears.

02

Pages Google cannot read

Duplicate titles, empty descriptions, images with no alt text. Google does not know what you sell, or where you work.

03

No page for each town, no page for each service

Your competitors have a page for “facade renovation in Rennes”. You have a page called “Our services”. That one detail costs you the top spot.

04

Plugins working against each other

Three SEO plugins added over the years, two caches competing, a sitemap pointing to deleted pages. The site sabotages itself.

§ 05 — Objections

What we are asked before signing

How long before I see results?

The technical gains — speed, indexing — show within two to three weeks. Rankings for local search terms usually move between day forty-five and day ninety. The audit gives you a dated estimate, not a vague promise.

Are you going to break my site?

A full backup, files and database, is taken before every piece of work and kept for thirty days. Changes are applied to a test copy first. If something goes wrong, restoring it takes under ten minutes, and we do not charge for it.

I already have an agency looking after this.

Then ask them for your last three ranking reports. If they exist and show progress, change nothing. If they do not exist, the free audit will tell you within forty-eight hours what is actually being done to your site.

My trade is very specialised. You do not know it.

True — and that is why the first step is a thirty-minute conversation where you tell us who calls you, what for, and what makes them pick you. It is your words, not ours, that end up on your pages.

What if I want to stop?

One email stops the monthly work, with no notice period and no penalty. Everything produced — pages, settings, access — belongs to you and stays where it is.
